### 1. Hero / page opener — bridging old and new wellness
**Placement:** Below the hero intro paragraph (before the H2 "My Story"), as the page's first visual anchor.
**Filename:** `about-japanese-wellness-personal-journey-senior-tea-window.jpg`
**Prompt:**
> Photorealistic photograph, 16:9 aspect ratio. A senior person around 58, short grey-brown hair, sitting calmly at a low wooden table near a tall window in a minimalist room. Both hands cradle a small ceramic teacup of green tea, posture relaxed but upright, head slightly tilted in soft thought. Eyes lowered toward the cup, gentle half-smile. Cream walls, a single small houseplant on the windowsill, a worn paperback book face-down on the table, a folded grey linen throw on a bench in soft focus. Warm late-morning light enters from the right, casting a soft glow on the side of the face. He wears a heather-grey crew-neck sweater over a cream cotton shirt. Reflective, grounded, honest mood — the personal, lived quality of the brand. Natural authentic photography, shallow depth of field, no commercial gloss.
